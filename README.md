# OSINT Hub

### Open-Source Intelligence & Cybersecurity Research Platform

**OSINT Hub** is a centralized open-source intelligence platform built for cybersecurity researchers, security professionals, investigators, analysts, and intelligence enthusiasts.

The platform provides a structured environment for discovering, organizing, and analyzing publicly available information across the open internet. OSINT Hub brings together investigative resources, reconnaissance techniques, research tools, and intelligence workflows into a single, accessible platform.

### Intelligence. Research. Reconnaissance.

OSINT Hub is designed to simplify the process of gathering and correlating publicly available intelligence. From digital footprint analysis and username discovery to domain reconnaissance and online presence research, the platform provides resources that support a wide range of cybersecurity and investigative operations.

### Core Capabilities

**Digital Footprint Intelligence**
Research publicly available information associated with online identities, usernames, domains, and digital assets.

**Reconnaissance & Discovery**
Discover publicly exposed information and identify relevant digital infrastructure during authorized security assessments.

**OSINT Resources**
Access a curated collection of tools, techniques, references, and resources for open-source intelligence research.

**Security Research**
Support cybersecurity learning, threat research, reconnaissance, and defensive intelligence activities through publicly available data.

**Investigation Workflow**
Organize research into a structured workflow, allowing investigators and researchers to efficiently move from discovery to analysis.

### Built for Security Researchers

OSINT Hub is designed with cybersecurity research and responsible intelligence gathering in mind. The platform emphasizes publicly accessible information and ethical investigation practices, making it suitable for security education, authorized assessments, threat intelligence research, and legitimate digital investigations.
